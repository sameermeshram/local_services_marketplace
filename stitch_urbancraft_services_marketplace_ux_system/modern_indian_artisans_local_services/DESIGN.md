## Brand & Style
The design system powers an on-demand verified marketplace connecting modern Indian households with vetted professionals, technicians, and local trade masters. The brand narrative merges uncompromising professional trust with warm, hospitable accessibility. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Refined Modern Utility**: a synthesis of utilitarian precision (inspired by fintech leaders), domestic warmth, and marketplace clarity. Visual elements balance crisp architectural density with generous breathing room. Trust is not assumed—it is visually certified through dedicated high-fidelity badges, luminous micro-accents, clear typography hierarchy, and tangible depth. 

Interfaces reject generic consumer clutter in favor of calm, intentional layouts where pricing transparency, professional credentials, and service SLAs command prime visual real estate.

## Colors
The system is built upon a high-contrast, foundational palette anchored by authoritative Slate Navy (`#0F172A`) and Deep Obsidian (`#0A0D0C`), delivering immediate legibility and institutional trust across dense urban and tier-1/tier-2 Indian viewing conditions.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`):** Anchors high-priority headers, primary action elements, key navigational nodes, and prominent framing.
- **Secondary (`#059669`):** The operational accent. Drives primary conversion paths, booking confirmations, immediate call-to-actions, and positive availability states.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`):** Applied selectively for digital telemetry, warranty guarantees, tracking states, and verified service tier badges.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Governs secondary metadata, supporting body copy, and peripheral captions.
- **Specialized Tokens:** `rating-amber` (`#F59E0B`) is reserved strictly for aggregate reviews and trust metrics. `badge-emerald-tint` (`#E8F5EE`) acts as the background foil for verified partner checks and certification pips.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is selected across all roles to achieve a blend of contemporary geometric clarity, humanist warmth, and razor-sharp readability on mobile displays.

- **Display & Headlines:** Employs negative letter spacing and heavy weights (700/800) to create clear entry points into service categories and verified guarantees.
- **Body:** Uses balanced line heights (1.5–1.6x) and regular weight (400) to ensure high readability for service scopes, terms, and technician bios.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Features tighter sizing combined with increased tracking and bold weights (600/700) to optimize glanceable badges, price tags, status chips, and ratings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system enforces an 8-point spatial rhythm tailored for fluid responsiveness across varied device form factors.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max content boundary capped at 1280px, with `margin` of 32px (`2rem`) and `gutter` of 24px (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with dynamic gutters matching the 16px/24px step.
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column responsive grid with a strict 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margin to maximize actionable surface area for booking flows.

Component internal layouts follow direct multiples of 4px and 8px: `space-xs` (4px) for badge text-to-icon gaps, `space-sm` (8px) for input padding and rating clusters, `space-md` (16px) for standard modular separation, and `space-xl` (32px) for card interior padding on desktop viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated via structured surface layering combined with ambient, diffused shadows tinted by deep charcoal slate (`#0F172A`), avoiding generic muddy black shadows.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`), pure non-reflective backdrop.
- **Card Tier (Level 1):** `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px perimeter border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive / Hover Tier (Level 2):** Elevated interactive cards, floating quick-book panels, and search bars: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, shifting the border tone to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Overlay Tier (Level 3):** Modal sheets, bottom drawer booking confirmations, and sticky navigation headers: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Sticky mobile navigation incorporates a light background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with 90% opacity over `surface-card`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy favors calibrated modern curves (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting approachable hospitality without sacrificing structural discipline.

- **Base Controls & Buttons:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, inputs, and form controls, providing clear interactive touch targets.
- **Content Cards & Service Blocks:** Configured at `1rem` (16px) corner radius to create inviting, self-contained interactive modules.
- **Badges, Status Chips, & Trust Pips:** Full pill geometry (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate informative metadata from actionable rectangular controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Emerald (`#059669`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, 8px corner radius. Heights: 44px (mobile), 48px (desktop). Transitions seamlessly to `#047857` on hover.
- **Secondary Action:** Slate Navy (`#0F172A`) fill with white text for critical confirmation points.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** 1.5px solid border (`#CBD5E1`), background transparent, text `#0F172A`. On hover, fills with `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive:** Border and text `#DC2626`, hover background `#FEF2F2`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Features integrated price summaries, warranty badges, and service duration tags (`space-sm` gap).
- Hover transforms smoothly with a 2px upward translation (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by Level 2 shadow expansion.

### Trust & Verification Badges
- **Verified Pro Badge:** Pill container, `#E8F5EE` background, 1px `#A7F3D0` border, `#059669` text and verification checkmark icon.
- **Rating Indicator:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#F59E0B` icon and typography, displaying review count in muted `#64748B`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height 48px, 8px corner radius, `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Focus state activates a 2px `#059669` ring with an offset of 1px, eliminating generic browser outlines.
- Clear inline labels with `label-md` styling and dedicated helper/error text slots positioned 4px beneath the container.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 20x20px, 4px corner radius for checkboxes, 50% circular for radios.
- Unselected state uses 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border. Active checked state triggers `#059669` fill with crisp white vector marks.

### Bottom Sheet & Mobile Drawer (Marketplace Specific)
- Anchored to bottom screen edges on mobile devices for checkout, slot picking, and professional details.
- 20px top-left and top-right radii, Level 3 elevation, featuring an accessible 36x4px drag pill at the top header margin.
